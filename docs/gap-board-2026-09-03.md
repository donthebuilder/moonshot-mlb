# The gap board — built, and the doubles score killed by its own test

Date: 2026-09-03 · `components/tabs/GapBoard.js`, `lib/triples.js`,
`components/tabs/HitsHRR.js`, `components/Dashboard.js`

---

## Start with the part I got wrong

This morning I recommended scoring this board on **doubles**, on the grounds
that triples had 27 graded events and doubles had 385 — fourteen times the
sample, same construction, same fields. You said do it. So I built it and tested
it against the archive before shipping any of it.

**It does not work.** Tested on 2,297 graded player-nights:

| | top-decile lift | z |
|---|---|---|
| doubles composite | **0.76×** base rate | −1.46 |
| — first half of the season | 0.86× | −0.57 |
| — second half | 0.59× | −1.93 |
| best single term (`recent_ld_rate`) | 1.22× | +1.34 |
| `hr_score` vs doubles | 0.70× | — |

And the control that settles it: **a random score's top decile lands between
0.78× and 1.25× ninety-five times out of a hundred.** So the best single term on
the board sits *inside* the noise band, and the composite is worse than picking
names out of a hat — consistently, in both halves of the season.

`hr_score` scoring 0.70× against doubles is not a bug, incidentally. A ball that
leaves the yard is not a double. The two markets compete for the same swings.

**The event count was never the problem. The signal is not in these fields.**
That is a different and more useful finding than "not enough data yet," because
more nights of the same fields will not fix it.

## So the board ships with no score, and says so

`components/tabs/GapBoard.js` does what the steal board did on day one: it ranks
on counts and rates the bot published, prints the denominator beside every rate,
and makes no claim it cannot support. A banner at the top states the 0.76×
result in words — because a board with no score, sitting beside four boards that
have one, reads as a board whose score failed to load unless it says otherwise.

**Columns**, all live tonight: season 3B · 3B per 600 PA · 2B · XBH · line-drive
rate · legs · gap depth · the opposing starter's LD% and ISO against · **2B and
3B prices** (over 0.5, with movement from open in the tooltip).

**Nine sorts**, triples first. "Real gap bats" (10+ XBH) on by default — 213 of
tonight's 261; the 48 hidden are stated on the button.

**The PA floor blanks, it doesn't zero.** Under 250 PA the per-600 rate prints
an em-dash with the reason in its tooltip, on 41 of 213 rows tonight. A blank
cell is a true statement; a small number is a false one.

### The three refusals, written into the file

1. **No score, for either market.** Not "not yet" — pending a feature nobody has
   tried, not more nights of these.
2. **No park triples factor.** The slate has park factors for HR, hits,
   distance, barrels, hard-hit and K, and none for extra-base hits. The GAPS
   column is real outfield geometry instead — `(LCF+RCF)/2 − (LF+RF)/2` — which
   ranks tonight Fenway +94, PNC +60, Kauffman +57, Dodger +55. That is the true
   triples leaderboard, and it is still labelled a proxy.
3. **No pitcher XBH rate.** `pitcher_xbh_vs_lhb/rhb` are raw counts (0–43) with
   no batters-faced field anywhere in the payload, so ranking by them ranks
   innings pitched. The ARM column shows his LD rate and ISO against, both
   already rates.

`lib/triples.js` now carries the full test result in its header and is **wired to
nothing** — kept as the record of an idea that was tried and did not work.

## Where it lives

A **Gap** group inside the boards page beside Steals — no new nav entry, same
decision and same reason as the steal board. Reachable at **`#tab=gap`** and
**`#tab=triples`** (both route there: triples is what you asked for, gap is what
it turned out to be).

## The three bot asks are unchanged and still outstanding

They live in `MLB-HR-DASHBOARD-STREAMLIT`, which isn't checked out here — this
repo is read-only and only consumes its `data` branch.

1. **`pitcher_bf`** — turns the XBH counts into a rate and lets refusal #3 come off.
2. **`park_3b_factor`** — a real season triples factor, replacing the geometry proxy.
3. **Grade the whole 261-row slate, not just the ~90 picks** — worth doing on its
   own merits for every market, though on today's evidence it will not rescue a
   doubles score.
