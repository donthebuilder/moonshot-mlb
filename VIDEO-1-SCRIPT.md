# Video 1 — loose script

Not a word-for-word read. Beats, in order, with the line that matters at each
one. Say the rest however you say it.

**Premise:** everyone posts picks, nobody posts whether they were right. You
open with the grade, then earn the picks.

**Start on: Results → Day picker → yesterday.** Not the Games board. The site
now keeps nine graded days, so you have last night no matter when you record.

---

## Beat 1 — Cold open on last night's grade (45s)

Results tab. Click yesterday in the Day row.

> "This is what the board said last night, and what actually happened."

Point at the capture rate.

> "Thirty homers hit across the slate. This many were on my sheet. That's the
> number I care about, and it's the number nobody posts."

Don't editorialise it. If it was a bad night, say it was a bad night — that's
the entire reason this open works.

*Now* say who you are. One sentence.

---

## Beat 2 — What the board got right, and what it missed (90s)

Still on yesterday. Overview sub-tab.

Show the HR scorers. Then scroll to **Missed HRs**.

> "These went deep and weren't on my sheet. I'd rather show you these than
> pretend the misses don't exist."

That's the trust beat of the whole video. Don't rush it.

Then the top-5 longest.

> "Longest of the night. Five, not thirty — past the top handful the distances
> stop being interesting."

---

## Beat 3 — Why the numbers mean anything (60s)

This is where you separate from every other account. Pools tab.

> "When this says 18.7%, that's not a model score dressed up as a percentage.
> That's what hitters in that score band actually did over thirty-four graded
> days."

Say the honest part immediately after:

> "Thirty-four days isn't a lot. I'm not promising you a hit rate. I'm showing
> you what's been observed and letting you decide."

That sentence is worth more than any pick in the video.

---

## Beat 4 — Tonight's slate (3 min)

Now switch to Games. You've earned it.

Pick one game, open one hitter. Go **Pitcher tab first**, not the lineup.

> "Start with the arm. His mix against this side — not his overall usage,
> because those look nothing alike."

Order-thirds table:

> "I use this, not the lineup-spot table under it. That one splits a season nine
> ways, about twelve plate appearances a row. Twelve."

Then **Pitch** tab — sort by BBE on camera.

> "Sort by balls in play first. That tells you which of these lines is real and
> which is one swing."

Then **Spray**.

> "Where he actually puts it. Position is where it was fielded, not how far it
> carried. And these pitch chips come up pre-set to what tonight's guy throws."

Hit L10 and point at the counter.

> "Ten games, about twenty-seven batted balls. L5 is around fourteen and I don't
> trust the shape at fourteen."

---

## Beat 5 — Your three names (60s)

> "Three names, and a reason for each that isn't a score."

For each one, one sentence with a number and its sample. Not "the model likes
him."

If lineups aren't out yet:

> "Lineups aren't posted. Check before you do anything with this — projected
> leadoff guys get scratched."

---

## Beat 6 — Close (30s)

> "Tomorrow I'll show you how these three did, same as I just did with last
> night. That's the whole channel."

That's your hook for video 2 and it costs you nothing.

---

## Rules for the whole thing

- **Every rate gets its sample out loud.** The site prints it next to every
  number. Skip it once and you're every other account.
- **Never "the model likes him."** Say what it's reading.
- **Never promise a hit rate.** Thirty-four days. Someone will screenshot it.
- **Show a miss on purpose.** Every video. It's the differentiator.

## Before you record

- Results → Day picker → yesterday, confirm it loads.
- One game and one hitter picked so you're not scrolling and reading.
- **Don't open Hot Zones.** Empty until the bot change lands.
- If recording early, skip the ⭐ SPOT column and the lineup-spot table — the
  order is projected and that's the one thing not locked.
